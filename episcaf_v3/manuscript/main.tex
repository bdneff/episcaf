\noindent
This is the running record of a new design avenue for episcaf, kept as its own subproject
(\texttt{episcaf\_v3/}) inside the episcaf repository. The shipped method at the repository root
(``v2'') scaffolds antibody epitopes defined by 3D \emph{geometry}; this log asks
whether defining the epitope by \emph{energy} --- which residues actually carry the binding --- and
letting the design constraints follow the energetics produces better scaffolds. It is built one
recorded decision at a time (\texttt{docs/DECISIONS.md}) and is meant to be read in order: each
section is one iteration of what we tried, what we found, and what it changed for the next step. It
is not a formal manuscript, and the method here is early --- most of it is still to build.
