Design protein scaffolds that display a selected epitope

episcaf builds small proteins around selected antigen residues. It evaluates how closely the predicted structures preserve the epitope and whether surrounding scaffold residues could obstruct antibody binding.

Pipeline: RFdiffusion3 → ProteinMPNN → AlphaFold3, then scoring on epitope fidelity and antibody accessibility.

Scaffold a selected epitope

Antigen (grey) with the selected epitope in red.

Start from an antibody–antigen complex; episcaf takes the epitope from the antibody contact residues. Generate scaffold designs and compare their predicted structures with the original epitope. For an antigen with no bound antibody, the epitope can instead be read off the structure.

Use this when: you have a complex (for example a SAbDab entry) or a specific site you want displayed on a stable, synthesizable scaffold.

You can also mark the antibody chains or individual residues yourself; episcaf derives the antigen contact epitope from the selection.
