Applications

Map antibody recognition

Design scaffold libraries representing different antigen regions to investigate which sites monoclonal antibodies or serum antibodies recognize.

Compare epitope variants

Use scaffold variants to investigate how changes at selected residues affect antibody binding.

Develop defined assay reagents

Design scaffolds displaying individual antigen sites for evaluation as capture, detection, or competition reagents.

Explore epitope-focused immunogens

Develop scaffold candidates that present selected antigen sites for experimental evaluation in immunogen design.
