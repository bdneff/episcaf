Design protein scaffolds that display a selected epitope

episcaf builds small proteins around selected antigen residues. It evaluates how closely the predicted structures preserve the epitope and whether the surrounding scaffold could obstruct antibody binding.

Backbones are generated with RFdiffusion3, sequences are designed with ProteinMPNN, and structures are predicted with AlphaFold 3. The resulting designs are scored and ranked.
