Run growPatch on your antigen

Upload an antigen structure (PDB). growPatch enumerates candidate conformational epitopes over its surface — the exhaustive “what could an antibody target” list, not a single prediction. Pick a candidate to highlight it; drag to rotate; switch to the flat surface map. This page needs the local server (python scripts/growpatch_serve.py) — it runs the real enumerator on your upload.
