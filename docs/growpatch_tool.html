Sequence segments and gap filling. A candidate can contain several sequence segments that are adjacent in the folded antigen. growPatch fills short sequence gaps so the scaffold includes the backbone connecting nearby surface residues: the segments sent to scaffold design are the gap-filled ranges, and candidates are ranked by the total DiscoTope score over those residues. Structure processing does not establish that an antigen or candidate region is suitable for the intended experiment. Preparing design inputs writes the design specifications and run configuration; it does not start RFdiffusion3, ProteinMPNN, or AlphaFold 3. Data source: episcaf_analysis/growpatch.
