Run growPatch on your antigen

See the 8PWW demo if you just want to look. This page runs the real enumerator on your upload and needs the local service.

Upload an antigen structure (PDB). growPatch samples candidate surface patches that could be antibody footprints; it does not predict which regions are epitopes. Pick a candidate to highlight it, drag to rotate, or switch to the projection map. This page runs the real enumerator on your upload, so it requires the local service (python scripts/growpatch_serve.py).

Helicity bias is exploratory and not yet wired into generation: helical designs may bind better — or may just be better behaved in cryo-EM, so the correlation can be a reporting artifact rather than a true binding effect. The slider runs −1 (away from helix) … 0 (neutral) … +1 (toward helix); leave it at 0 for now.
Notes. A candidate patch can contain residues from separate stretches of the amino acid sequence that lie next to one another in the folded protein (sequence segments). The projection map places each residue by its angle about the chain centroid, shaded by relative solvent accessibility (light = buried → blue = exposed); it is a rough overview, not a surface unwrap, and its 2D distances are not molecular distances. Whether an uploaded antigen is actually suitable for episcaf is not yet checked. Candidates come from the enumerator (episcaf_analysis/growpatch); the method tag is provenance, not a confidence score: Found by both methods was produced by both, Neighbor-based growth and Directional sampling by one method only. "Prepare design inputs" writes a contigs CSV and run spec for RFdiffusion3→ProteinMPNN→AlphaFold3; it does not start the design job.
