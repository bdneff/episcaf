Candidate surface regions: 8PWW

Process your own antigen → Processing a new structure requires the local service.

This example shows candidate regions generated for PfRH5, chain A of 8PWW. growPatch groups nearby exposed surface residues into candidate sites, which are ranked by their mean DiscoTope score. Select a candidate to highlight its residues; drag the structure to rotate it.

Candidate generation identifies regions for design and screening; it does not establish antibody binding.

Candidates are ranked by the mean DiscoTope-3.0 score over their growth-selected surface residues (the residues growPatch grew the patch from, before gap filling). Scores are normalized within this antigen, so they give a relative ranking rather than a calibrated binding probability.

Sequence segments and gap filling. A candidate can contain several sequence segments that are adjacent in the folded antigen. growPatch fills short sequence gaps so the scaffold includes the backbone connecting nearby surface residues: the segments sent to scaffold design are the gap-filled ranges, while the surface residues used for ranking are the growth-selected residues only. Data source: scripts/growpatch_viz_data.py.
