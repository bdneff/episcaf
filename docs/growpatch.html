growPatch — candidate epitope viewer

This is a fixed 8PWW demo — run growPatch on your own antigen → (needs the local server).

Without a bound antibody there is no known binding site, so growPatch samples candidate surface patches across the antigen that could be antibody footprints. You can then scaffold the candidates and screen them. It does not predict which regions are epitopes (that is the task of tools such as DiscoTope and BepiPred). Two methods are combined: neighbor-based patch growth and directional surface sampling. Each candidate records which method or methods produced it. Candidates can be ranked by predicted epitope likelihood (DiscoTope-3.0): the per-residue B-cell epitope likelihood DiscoTope-3.0 assigns to chain A, summarized per candidate as the mean over its residues (size-independent; the sum is also offered). This is a structure-based prediction, separate from the geometric surface sampling that generated the candidates, and it is not a measurement of antibody binding. Scores are normalized 0–1 across the chain.

How to read this. Grey is the antigen; a selected candidate's residues are red. Sequence segments: a patch can contain residues from separate stretches of the amino acid sequence that lie next to one another in the folded protein, so one candidate may span more than one segment. The projection map places each residue by its angle about the chain centroid, shaded by relative solvent accessibility (light = buried → blue = exposed); it is a rough overview of where a patch sits, not a surface unwrap, and its 2D distances are not molecular distances. The method tag on each candidate is provenance, not a confidence score: Found by both methods was produced by both, Neighbor-based growth and Directional sampling by one method only.
