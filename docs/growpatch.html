growPatch — candidate epitope viewer

With no antibody, there is no known epitope. growPatch does not predict one (that is DiscoTope / BepiPred); it enumerates every surface region that could plausibly be an antibody footprint, so you can scaffold them all and let the experiment decide. Two complementary strategies — seed-and-grow and the antibody’s-eye-view direction caps — are unioned. Below is the real output on 8PWW chain A; click a candidate to highlight it, drag to rotate, or switch to the flat-surface map.

How to read this. Grey is the antigen; a selected candidate's residues are red. The flat-surface map is an equirectangular projection of the chain around its centroid (each dot a residue, shaded by relative SASA, light = buried → blue = exposed) — a lossy overview for seeing where a patch sits on the whole surface, not a faithful metric space. Patches tagged both were found by both strategies (higher confidence); grow and eye are method-exclusive. Data regenerated by scripts/growpatch_viz_data.py.
