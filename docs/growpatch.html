About this example and its metrics

The residue map plots angular positions around the antigen centroid. Distances on the map are separate from molecular distances.

Mean DiscoTope scores reduce the direct effect of candidate size. Sum scores reflect the number of contributing residues. Scores are normalized within this antigen and represent relative scores.

Sequence segments. A candidate can contain several sequence segments that are adjacent in the folded antigen, so one candidate may span more than one segment. Method labels identify how a candidate was generated; agreement between methods is not a binding-confidence score (Found by both methods was produced by both, Neighbor-based growth and Directional sampling by one method only). Data source: scripts/growpatch_viz_data.py.
