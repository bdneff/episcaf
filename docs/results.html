No results loaded. Load a run's metrics CSV to rank and filter designs. The viewer shows whichever metric columns are present; canonical column names and their meanings are in the results schema.

Expected columns include design_id, epitope_chunk_rmsd (epitope backbone RMSD to native, Å), overall_rmsd (Å), mean_pae (Å), af3_n_clash_res (measured antibody clashes) or aomclash_pred (model estimate), and a composite or binding score. Everything is processed in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

Reading it. Each design is pass, fail, or not evaluated. A green left-edge marks a pass: it clears all four filters — epitope RMSD ≤ 1 Å, overall RMSD ≤ 2 Å, mean PAE < 5, and no antibody clash. Clash uses the measured count af3_n_clash_res (must be 0) when a reference antibody was present, otherwise the model estimate aomclash_pred (must be < 3); these are different quantities. A grey left-edge marks not evaluated — a required metric, including any clash measurement, is missing, so the design is not counted as a pass. Click a column header to sort; it defaults to composite (then epitope fidelity). Passing these filters does not establish experimental binding. See RESULTS_SCHEMA.md.
