How structural filters are evaluated
Pass: the design meets every required threshold. Fail: the available metrics show that the design does not meet the configured criteria. Not evaluated: at least one required metric is missing. The thresholds are epitope RMSD ≤ 1 Å, overall RMSD ≤ 2 Å, and mean PAE < 5 Å. For antibody obstruction, the viewer uses zero clashing residues against a reference antibody (af3_n_clash_res = 0) or, when none is available, a calibrated AOMclash estimate below 3 (aomclash_pred < 3). Filter status describes the structural screen; experimental binding is evaluated separately.
