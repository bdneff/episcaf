Supported columns

Common columns identify the design and epitope, structural deviations, prediction confidence, antibody clashes, and ranking scores. Metric columns can be omitted, but a design cannot be evaluated against a filter whose required metric is missing. Canonical column names and exact definitions are in the results-file format and metric definitions.

Structural-filter status. Pass: the design meets every required threshold (green left edge). Fail: the available metrics show that the design does not meet the configured criteria. Not evaluated: at least one required metric is missing (grey left edge). The thresholds are epitope RMSD ≤ 1 Å, overall RMSD ≤ 2 Å, and mean PAE < 5 Å. For antibody obstruction, the viewer uses zero clashing residues against a reference antibody (af3_n_clash_res = 0). A legacy aomclash_pred value alone leaves the screen unevaluated. Filter status describes the structural screen and experimental binding is evaluated separately. Select a column heading to sort by that metric. See RESULTS_SCHEMA.md.
