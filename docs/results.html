Design results

Load a run's metrics CSV (what the pipeline's stage 5 produces) to rank and filter designs by their AlphaFold3 metrics and composite score. Columns follow the results schema; the viewer shows whatever metric columns are present. Everything is in your browser — nothing is uploaded.

Reading it. A green left-edge marks a design that clears the four-filter (overall_rmsd≤2, epitope_chunk_rmsd≤1, mean_pae<5, clash 0). Click a column header to sort; it defaults to composite (then fidelity). Accessibility is af3_n_clash_res where an antibody was present, else aomclash_pred. See RESULTS_SCHEMA.md.
